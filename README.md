## Residential proxies: $6/GB down to $2.50/GB

The residential ladder steps down four times: $6/GB up to about 2 GB, $5/GB up to 20 GB, $4/GB up to 125 GB, then $2.50/GB from 1K+ GB on Enterprise. The entry subscription is billed at $6/GB; confirm the minimum monthly charge on the order page, because Oxylabs' plan cards and third-party tallies of the same page do not agree on that number.

What the $6/GB buys: a pool of 175M+ residential IPs across 195 countries, unlimited concurrent sessions, three proxy users, sticky sessions, HTTP(S), HTTP/3 and SOCKS5, 10 whitelisted IPs, and an average 99.95% success rate. Geo-targeting down to country, state, city, ZIP, coordinates and ASN is included rather than sold as an add-on, which is not universal among providers.

The waste problem sits in the tier logic. Rates are tied to the size of the allowance you buy, not to what you use. Need 10 GB? You pay $6/GB because you cannot reach the 20 GB tier without spending $100. Need 60 GB? You are paying $5/GB for 100 GB of allowance and leaving 40 GB to expire.

## Mobile proxies: $7.50/GB down to $3.50/GB

Mobile starts at $7.50/GB, drops to $6.67/GB up to 14 GB, $5.00/GB up to 100 GB, and $3.50/GB at 714+ GB on Enterprise. The pool is 20M+ mobile IPs in 140+ countries with 5G, 4G and LTE rotation.

If you have read an older Oxylabs review quoting $9/GB pay-as-you-go or Micro/Starter/Advanced bandwidth packs at $8.25, $7.89 and $7.50, that was the previous pricing structure. Those figures no longer match the current rate card, which is organised around fixed monthly credit rather than per-GB top-ups.

## ISP and dedicated ISP: $1.50/IP down to $1.10/IP

Shared ISP proxies, where up to three users share each IP, run $1.50/IP on the $5 welcome credit and the entry subscription, $1.40/IP up to 71 IPs, $1.20/IP up to 416 IPs, and $1.10/IP at 2.27K+ IPs.

Dedicated ISP starts at $3.30/IP for one to four IPs, falls to $2.78/IP up to 35 IPs, $2.60/IP up to 192 IPs, and $2.20/IP at 1.14K+ IPs. You get free premium ASN selection, full control of each IP, HTTP/HTTPS/SOCKS5, unlimited bandwidth under fair use, and sessions with no time limit.

Static ISP is the product line where the entry price tag misleads most. Three dollars and thirty cents per IP sounds trivial until you notice the minimum purchase is a monthly subscription, so a five-IP test is a $16.50 monthly commitment rather than a one-off charge.

## Datacenter proxies: two different meters

Dedicated datacenter is billed per IP: $2.00/IP on the welcome credit and entry plan, $1.75/IP up to 57 IPs, $1.45/IP up to 344 IPs, $1.20/IP at 2.08K+ IPs, with a pool of up to 1.9M IPs in 188 countries.

Shared datacenter comes in two flavours. Per IP, it runs $1.00/IP down to $0.60/IP at 4.17K+ IPs, drawn from a 36K-IP pool in 38 countries. Per gigabyte on rotating proxies, it runs $0.60/GB down to $0.40/GB at 6.25K+ GB, from a 56K-IP pool in 44 countries. The per-GB route is the only part of the Oxylabs catalogue that approaches commodity pricing, and it is also the part where the free trial is not available.

## Rules that only appear in the help centre

- **No true pay-as-you-go.** Beyond the one-time $5 welcome credit, self-service products require an active monthly plan.
- **Credit expires each cycle.** Unused allowance and top-ups do not roll forward unless you upgrade.
- **Self-service purchases are non-refundable.** Refunds on other plans depend on the conditions in the subscription agreement.
- **KYC for everyone.** Every customer completes a questionnaire, and Oxylabs states it rejects at least a quarter of customer enquiries each year.
- **The residential network blocks some targets,** including Apple domains, banking sites, some Google domains, government, streaming, ticketing and mailing sites. Check your target list before you buy bandwidth you cannot spend.
- **No cryptocurrency payments.** Cards, PayPal, Google Pay, Apple Pay and wire transfer are accepted.
- **No IPv6 product** appears on the pricing page.

## FAQ

**What is the minimum I have to spend on Oxylabs?** Beyond the free $5 welcome credit, self-service products need an active monthly plan, priced at $6/GB on the entry residential tier. Confirm the exact minimum monthly charge on the order page, since published tallies differ.

**Do unused credits roll over?** No. Plan allowance and top-ups expire at the end of each billing cycle and carry over only when you upgrade to a larger plan.

**Can I get a refund?** Self-service purchases are non-refundable. Other plans may qualify under the subscription agreement, so raise the question before paying rather than after.

**How far does the $5 welcome credit go?** About 0.8 GB of residential traffic at $6/GB, or roughly 0.7 GB of mobile at $7.50/GB. It requires no card and works across products.

**Why does Oxylabs ask for KYC?** Identity verification applies to every customer, and the company says it turns away at least a quarter of enquiries each year. Budget time for approval before a deadline-driven project.

**Why do some sites fail on residential proxies?** Oxylabs' residential network blocks certain categories, including Apple domains, banking, some Google domains, government, streaming, ticketing and mailing sites. Test those targets on the trial credit before buying volume.
